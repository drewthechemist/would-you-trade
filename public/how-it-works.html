How It Works

1. Find comparable sales

Our automated selection process uses documented sold listings from CompQuest. For a card to qualify, the system groups listings by player, year, set, card number, grading company, and grade. It requires at least three qualifying sales for that exact card identity.

2. Estimate market value

The system uses the median sale price, filters out sales substantially outside that median, and requires at least three remaining sales. It then estimates value from the median of up to ten recent qualifying sales. These are estimates, not guaranteed sale or trade prices.

3. Create a fair showdown

Two different players are matched only when their estimated values are within 15% of one another. The automated generator avoids reusing the same card identity within 30 days. If no eligible pair exists, it may not publish a new showdown that day.

4. Pick your card

Vote for the card you would rather own. After voting, you can view the results and compare your choice with other collectors. No account is required.

5. Understand the limits

The site attempts to limit voting to one vote per matchup per browser using a browser identifier. It is not a verified-person voting system, so results reflect participating browser votes, not a scientific survey. Card photos are sourced from listing images and may occasionally become unavailable.

Slab Showdown is entertainment and collector discussion, not investment, appraisal, or trading advice.
