About Slab Showdown

Slab Showdown is a daily game for sports-card collectors. Every showdown presents two graded cards with similar estimated market values and asks one simple question: Which would you rather own?

Instead of pairing cards at random, the game uses documented sold listings collected through CompQuest to build comparisons around observed market activity.

Built for the hobby

Card collecting is personal. Some collectors chase legendary players, others prefer modern rookies, favorite teams, or iconic sets. Slab Showdown gives collectors a place to compare those preferences and see how other visitors vote.

What the numbers mean

Displayed values are estimates based on qualifying sales, not price quotes or guarantees. Market prices change, and the value of an individual card depends on factors including its exact issue, grade, condition, and buyer demand.

Independent fan project

Slab Showdown is an independent collector-focused website. References to grading companies, players, card brands, and marketplaces are descriptive and do not imply affiliation or endorsement.
