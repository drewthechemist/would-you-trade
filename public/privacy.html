Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy describes how the Slab Showdown website is designed to handle information. The site operator should review and confirm the disclosures before publication, especially after enabling advertising or analytics.

Information used for voting

When you vote, the site creates or reads a randomly generated browser identifier stored in a cookie named wyt_device. It uses a one-way hash of that identifier and the matchup ID to help prevent repeat votes. The voting system stores the selected side and a vote timestamp, along with aggregate counts.

Technical information

Our hosting provider may process standard technical request information, such as IP addresses, device or browser details, and request logs, for service delivery, security, and troubleshooting. Contact us for questions about retention or access.

Card images and external links

Some card images are served from third-party listing hosts. Loading them may result in requests to those hosts. Links to third-party websites are governed by those websites' own privacy policies.

Advertising

We intend to use Google AdSense. Once advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver and measure ads, subject to applicable consent requirements and your settings. Learn more at Google's advertising privacy information. Advertising should not be enabled until appropriate consent and disclosure controls are in place where required.

Your choices

You can clear your browser cookies to remove the local voting identifier, although this does not delete already recorded votes. You may manage advertising choices through your browser and Google's available ad controls. For privacy requests, use our contact page.

Changes

This policy may be updated when site features, providers, or legal requirements change. The revision date will be updated accordingly.

Before launch, verify the hosting provider's retention practices, any analytics or consent-management tools, and the site's actual advertising configuration. This page is a starting draft, not legal advice.
